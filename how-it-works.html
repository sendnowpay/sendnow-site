How It Works

  1. Request. Submit shipment details through our form.
  2. Referral. With your consent, we forward your request to a licensed logistics partner.
  3. Quote. The partner — not SendNow — issues any quote, sets terms, and selects the carrier.
  4. Compare. We show you the partner's quote next to the price you pay today, including fuel, accessorial fees, transit time and liability limits.
  5. Book. If you accept, you book and pay through the partner directly.
  6. Track. The partner handles tracking, insurance, and claims.

How we are paid

SendNow is paid a referral or sales commission by the partner under a written agreement. You pay nothing to SendNow.

SendNow Logistics is a referral and marketing service. We are not a freight broker, freight forwarder, or motor carrier, and we do not arrange, quote, or perform transportation. All quotes, contracts, payments, insurance, and claims are handled by the licensed logistics partner named on your quote. Savings are not guaranteed.
