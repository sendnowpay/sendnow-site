What we collect

Business contact details and shipment details you submit (locations by ZIP, weight, dimensions, item type, dates, handling needs, and optionally your current price). We do not request payment cards, SSNs, or health information.

How we use it

To respond to your request, obtain quotes from licensed logistics partners, and send you messages about your request. We share shipment and contact details with a partner only after you consent on the form.

Automated tools

We use software, including AI tools, to organize requests and prepare comparisons. A person reviews customer-facing communications.

Email

You can opt out of marketing email at any time by replying "unsubscribe".

Retention and deletion

We keep request records for up to 3 years, or less if you ask us to delete them, unless the law requires otherwise. Email the contact address to request access or deletion.

We do not sell personal information.

SendNow Logistics is a referral and marketing service. We are not a freight broker, freight forwarder, or motor carrier, and we do not arrange, quote, or perform transportation. All quotes, contracts, payments, insurance, and claims are handled by the licensed logistics partner named on your quote. Savings are not guaranteed.
