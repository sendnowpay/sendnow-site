Parcel shipping software: Shippo

Shippo is multi-carrier shipping software for online sellers and small businesses. It provides discounted label rates from major carriers, label printing, and connections to e-commerce platforms. Its Starter plan has no monthly fee.

Pallets and LTL freight

If you ship one to six pallets between businesses, a licensed freight provider may be able to beat your current rate. Send us a recent shipment and we will request a comparison from a licensed partner.

Before you compare, have these ready

  • Pickup and delivery ZIP codes
  • Weight and dimensions (palletized, if applicable)
  • Freight type, plus liftgate or appointment needs
  • What you paid last time, including fuel and accessorial charges
